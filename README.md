## communities
- [Superteam India](https://x.com/SuperteamIN) - solana builder community with bounties, grants and IRL meetups
- [SheFi India](https://x.com/shefiindia) - india chapter of SheFi: events, updates and education for women in web3
- [Web3 Chennai](https://x.com/web3chennai) - growing the web3 ecosystem in chennai and tamil nadu
- [Vidarbha DAO](https://x.com/VidarbhaDao) - web3 folks from central india. small cities, big web3 dreams
- [Wagmi Tribe](https://x.com/Wagmi_Tribe) - inclusive web3 community to connect, learn and grow together

## ecosystem outposts
- [Arbitrum India](https://x.com/arbitrum_india) - official india outpost of the arbitrum community
- [Base India](https://x.com/BasedIndia) - official base community in india
- [Hyperliquid India](https://x.com/Hyperliquid_In) - independent community of hyperliquid founders, builders and users in india
- [Stellar India](https://x.com/IND_Stellar) - stellar ecosystem in india

## events & conferences
- [ETHIndia](https://x.com/ETHIndiaco) - hackathons, fellowships and grants for the indian ethereum community
- [ETHMumbai](https://x.com/ETHMumbai) - ethereum conference + hackathon in mumbai ([2026 recap](https://devfolio.co/blog/ethmumbai2026/))
- India Blockchain Week - bengaluru, december ([event listing](https://cointelegraph.com/events/events-page/india-blockchain-week-2))
- [Hack4Bengal](https://x.com/hack4bengal) - eastern india's largest hackathon community, kolkata
- [Hack In Hills](https://x.com/hackinhills) - build-and-ship hackathon in the mountains

## hackathon platforms & calendars

- [Reskilll: blockchain hackathons](https://reskilll.com/allhacks/c/blockchain) - another listing of blockchain hackathons

## policy & advocacy
- Bharat Web3 Association - industry body for india's crypto & web3 sector ([launch coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/03/coinbase-and-polygon-back-new-crypto-advocacy-group-in-india))

## reads & reports
- [Web3Bharat Weekly](https://paragraph.xyz/@w3bharat/web3bharat-weekly) - weekly newsletter on indian web3
- [India Ethereum Ecosystem Overview](https://localethereum.substack.com/p/india-ethereum-ecosystem-overview) - map of the indian ethereum scene
- [Polygon: Web3 Made in India tour](https://polygon.technology/blog/india-takes-center-stage-in-polygons-web3-made-in-india-tour) - city-by-city builder tour recap
- [india crypto launch playbook](https://github.com/0xkrishnac7/india-crypto-launch-playbook) - how to launch a crypto product in india on a small budget

## contribute
know a community, event or resource that belongs here? open a PR:
1. add it to the right section, alphabetical isn't required, quality is
2. format: `[name](link) - one line on what it is`
3. no token shills, no referral links, no dead projects
