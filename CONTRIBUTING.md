# contributing

thanks for helping keep this list useful 🙌

## what belongs here
- active indian web3 communities, ecosystem chapters and DAOs
- recurring events, conferences and hackathons in india
- platforms, calendars, newsletters, podcasts and reports about indian web3

## what doesn't
- token shills, presales, airdrops or referral links
- projects that haven't posted or shipped in 6+ months
- paid placements of any kind

## how to add something
1. fork the repo and edit `README.md`
2. add your entry to the right section: `[name](link) - one line on what it is`
3. open a PR with a one-line reason it belongs here

## found a dead link?
open an issue or a PR removing it. both are equally appreciated.
